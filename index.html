Clean Share Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026
Developer: ASAYA Media & Design LLC

The short version

Clean Share does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It cannot: the app does not request the internet permission, so it has no way to send anything off your device.

What the app does with your photos

When you choose photos in Clean Share, or share photos to it from another app, Clean Share reads those photos on your device, removes hidden metadata (such as GPS location, date and time, device make, model and serial numbers, embedded preview images, captions and editing data) and creates cleaned copies.

  • All processing happens locally on your device.
  • Cleaned copies are stored temporarily in the app's private cache. They are deleted when you tap "Start over" or the next time you clean photos. Android may also clear this cache at any time.
  • If you tap "Save", a cleaned copy is saved to your device's Pictures/Clean Share folder. You control those files.
  • If you tap "Share clean", the cleaned copies are passed to the app you choose. What that app does with them is governed by its own privacy policy.
  • Your original photos are never modified or deleted.

Data we collect

None. Clean Share has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting SDKs and no third-party trackers. It requests no Android permissions.

Backups

Clean Share opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so nothing from the app is copied to your Google account.

Children

Clean Share does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
